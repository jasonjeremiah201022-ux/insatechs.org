Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our courses and registration.

Who can attend INSATECH courses?

Our programmes are open to everyone – individuals, corporate staff, students and job seekers. Some advanced courses may require basic HSE knowledge or experience.

How do I apply for a course?

Go to the Courses page and click “Apply Now” on any course card. Choose email or phone and your app will open with details pre‑filled. You can also contact us directly via the details on the Contact page.

Where are the trainings held?

Most courses are conducted at our training facilities in Kaduna. On request, we can deliver in‑house training at your organisation’s premises, subject to minimum participant numbers.

Do you offer weekend or customised schedules?

Yes. INSATECH can arrange weekday, weekend or intensive schedules depending on group size and course type. Please contact us to discuss your preferred dates.

Will I receive a certificate?

Participants who successfully complete a course and meet attendance requirements receive an INSATECH certificate of completion for that programme.
