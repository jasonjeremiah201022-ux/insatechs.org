Courses

Explore INSATECH’s core Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) and technical skills programmes. Click “Apply Now” to contact us via email or phone.

HSE Level 1 – Foundation in Health, Safety & Environment

Introductory course covering basic HSE concepts, common workplace hazards and personal responsibility for safety. Ideal for new staff and students.

Duration: 2 days Mode: In-person Level: Beginner

HSE Level 2 – Workplace Risk Assessment

Builds on HSE Level 1 to focus on hazard identification, risk assessment and control measures in different work environments.

Duration: 3 days Mode: In-person Level: Intermediate

HSE Level 3 – Supervisory Safety Management

For supervisors and team leaders responsible for implementing safety procedures, incident reporting and promoting a positive safety culture.

Duration: 3 days Mode: In-person Level: Advanced

Basic Fire Safety & Extinguisher Use

Teaches causes of fire, classes of fire, extinguisher types and safe use, plus evacuation procedures for offices and industrial facilities.

Duration: 1 day Mode: In-person Practical drills

Advanced Firefighting & Emergency Response

Intensive training for emergency teams on incident command, firefighting tactics, equipment handling and coordinated response.

Duration: 3 days Mode: In-person For emergency teams

First Aid, CPR & AED Certification

Practical first aid course covering bleeding, shock, burns, fractures, CPR and use of Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs).

Duration: 2 days Mode: In-person Certification

Confined Space Entry & Rescue

Explains confined space hazards, entry permits, atmospheric testing and rescue procedures for tanks, pits and enclosed spaces.

Duration: 2 days Mode: In-person High‑risk work

Working at Height & Fall Protection

Covers safe use of ladders, scaffolds and fall‑arrest systems, including inspection, anchorage and rescue planning.

Duration: 2 days Mode: In-person Construction / maintenance

Electrical Safety for Non‑Electricians

Helps non‑technical staff understand electrical risks, lock‑out/tag‑out basics and safe practices around electrical equipment.

Duration: 1 day Mode: In-person For all staff

Process Safety & Hazard Analysis (HAZOP Essentials)

Introduces process safety concepts, basic hazard and operability (HAZOP) techniques and layers of protection in process industries.

Duration: 3 days Mode: In-person Technical personnel

Industrial Equipment Operation & Safety

Safety‑focused orientation for operators and supervisors working with forklifts, lifting equipment and other industrial machinery.

Duration: 2–3 days Mode: In-person Practical focus

General Safety Induction for New Employees

Short induction course for new hires, covering basic safety rules, emergency procedures and reporting channels in the workplace.

Duration: 1 day Mode: In-person All industries
